
# TuskeAI Vision

Technology should not replace people.
It should empower them.

Our goal is to create a modular, privacy-first AI system that grows together with its user, adapts over time, and becomes a trusted long-term companion.

TuskeAI is designed to be more than a chatbot.
It is a living project focused on collaboration, craftsmanship, openness, and continuous improvement.

We believe that great software is built with purpose, trust, and respect for the people who use it.

## Core Principles

- Human first
- Privacy by design
- Modular architecture
- Offline-first whenever possible
- Open collaboration
- Long-term sustainability
